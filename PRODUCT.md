# Solvaa

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People planning kayaking, canoeing and SUP outings, including trips over several days.

## Product Purpose

Discover useful places on a map, plot a paddle manually, and keep a useful day-by-day trip with food, camping and accommodation stops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Production requires email/password authentication through Supabase, without a Google account. Two-step onboarding captures approximate home and self-assessed experience. Optional screenshots and GPX/TCX/Strava CSV imports support personal evidence; distance does not certify technical competence. Supabase synchronizes profile and saved routes. Drafts, photos and imported session files stay on the device. Vercel serves the website and Express API. AI is optional, off by default and protected by durable account/IP/global quotas.

Launch suggestions are approximate; live points come from OpenStreetMap, with provider failures shown honestly. Operator-directory links are not given invented coordinates. Users choose each route point. Straight segments may still cross land; the app makes no claim of water validation. Manual GPX exports require an acknowledgement and contain separate tracks for each day. Earlier catalog outlines remain unexportable and their saved notes are preserved.

## Brand Commitments

Solvaa. The user requested Slopes as the design reference: map prominence, clear activity statistics, restrained controls and clear navigation.

## Product Principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Let people choose every leg; never invent navigable geometry.
- Keep every day and transfer explicit.
- Show the source of map places and uploaded evidence.
- Make personal evidence useful without overstating ability.
- Complete the phone flow with readable forms and touch-friendly controls.
- Explain what saves locally and what synchronizes to an account.
